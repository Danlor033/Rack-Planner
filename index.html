4KB Qty from JSTERP, every 30 seconds

JSTERP only shows stock to someone signed in, so the numbers are read by a Chrome extension on the office laptops. Any of them with Chrome open and signed in to JSTERP keeps the numbers updated for everyone, with no JSTERP tab open. One syncs at a time; if it stops, another takes over within a minute.

If this line turns red:

  1. Open Chrome on a laptop that has the extension.
  2. If it says signed out, sign in to JSTERP in that Chrome. The next run, within 30 seconds, picks it up.

Each laptop, its JSTERP account and whether it is syncing are on the Accounts page, which also has the extension to install on another laptop.

It reads Zone Inventory with owner Shigetsu, Main Warehouse, start position 4KB, and adds each SKU’s locations together. A SKU with nothing there gets 0.

4KB sync laptops

Add a laptop
  1. Click Download the extension and unzip it into a folder that stays put, such as Documents\rack-planner-4kb-sync. Chrome reads the extension from there.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode (top right), click Load unpacked and choose that folder.
  3. Sign in to JSTERP in the same Chrome.
  4. Within 30 seconds the laptop appears here as Laptop N. Rename it, and approve it so it keeps syncing once sign-in is required.

To update a laptop marked update: download again, replace the files in its folder, and click the reload arrow on the extension in chrome://extensions.

Sign in to Rack Planner

Rack Planner needs an account. Ask an admin if you do not have one.

Forgot your password?

Enter your email. The admins see your request on the Accounts page; one of them sets a new password and tells you. You can change it after signing in.
